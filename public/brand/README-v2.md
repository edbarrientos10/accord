## Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design a NEW professionally crafted Accord logo, using the attached three-concept brand reference as visual inspiration. The reference is a STYLE REFERENCE, not an image to reproduce. Most closely follow concept 01's confident diagonal folded geometry and concept 02's sculpted positive/negative space. Accord coordinates shared work between a person and their agents; ownership, purposeful handoff, mutual clarity.
Create ONE excellent finished horizontal logo on pure white. On the left a distinctive compact abstract folded mark: two broad ribbon-like planes, one warm vermilion #f04c32 and one deep ink #171b2b, arranged with a rising diagonal gesture and a deliberate open central counter. The geometry should feel like a single intelligent folded structure with two participants, not separate random shapes. Sculpted generous curves on outer turns, crisp diagonal interior cuts, carefully balanced weight and strong silhouette. Flatter and more economical than origami illustration. Allow a subtle abstract A through negative space, never a literal A icon. Original geometry; do not trace the reference. Do not use the previous blue lowercase-a stepped mark.
To the right the exact word "Accord", capital A and lowercase c c o r d, in deep ink. Refined medium-weight contemporary sans-serif with open rounded forms and considered optical kerning, resembling the elegant typography of the reference, NOT heavy rounded bubble lettering. Mark about 1.3 times capital letter height, generous but connected spacing. Composition fills a wide horizontal canvas with comfortable margin. Flat solid fills, crisp vector-like shapes. NO shadows, gradients, texture, glow, faux 3D, lettering inside the mark, labels, numbering, tagline or mockup. Avoid generic chain/infinity symbols, handshakes, puzzle pieces, recycling symbols, AI stars, checkmarks. Deliver just one large clean horizontal logo asset.

## Site asset preparation

The built-in image_gen tool produced the dark version using this final prompt:

Create the dark-background variant of this exact supplied Accord logo. Preserve geometry and lettering exactly. Change all navy areas (wordmark and dark portions of the folded symbol) to pure white. Keep the red section vermilion. Entire background including all counters and spaces MUST be perfectly uniform pure black #000000. OPAQUE black background, absolutely no transparency, no alpha removal, no grain, no glow, no shadow, no texture. Solid clean sharp logo, professionally flat 2D. Close crop around complete horizontal logo with modest equal padding. Text exactly Accord.

Dark and light logos were trimmed and exported to 960px WebP with Sharp. The dark logo uses CSS screen blending to integrate its black background into the site's charcoal surfaces. The browser also serves the v2 ICO at /favicon.ico.
